About Nano Shipbroking

Independent by approach. Connected by communication.

A cargo broking intermediary based in Istanbul, focused on connecting enquiries with relevant shipping market contacts.

Who we are

Building connections across the shipping market.

Nano Shipbroking is an independent cargo broking initiative based in Istanbul, Türkiye. Its activity centres on cargo enquiries, vessel availability research and communication between relevant cargo and shipping contacts.

The role is intermediary: helping bring information to the right parties, following up on outstanding details and exploring whether a cargo and vessel opportunity may be suitable to discuss further.

Nano Shipbroking does not represent itself as a vessel owner or fleet operator.

Our approach

Clarity, responsiveness and honest communication.

Shipping enquiries depend on accurate details and confirmation from the parties involved. We aim to keep communication direct and to distinguish an initial enquiry from a confirmed commercial arrangement.

01 /

Clear Details

Focus on the essential cargo, route, timing and vessel information.

02 /

Relevant Contacts

Communicate with market participants whose interests may fit the enquiry.

03 /

Honest Status

Do not describe a vessel, rate or fixture as confirmed without confirmation.
