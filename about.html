The company approach

Good business begins with a clear conversation.

Nano Shipbroking is an independent maritime broking initiative focused on connecting shipping enquiries with relevant market participants. Based in Istanbul, we work across cargo and vessel matching, chartering enquiries, and developing bunker broking activity.

Our role is commercial and intermediary: clarify the requirement, identify potential contacts and help maintain practical communication between the people involved.

We value responsiveness, straightforward details and an accurate understanding of what has — and has not — been confirmed.
