Services

What we do,
and how we help.

Independent commercial support across cargo enquiries, vessel opportunities and marine fuel quotation discussions.

SERVICE / 01

Cargo & Chartering Enquiries

Supporting discussions around cargo movements by collecting the commercial and operational facts needed to evaluate a potential match. Our role is to connect relevant cargo and vessel interests and facilitate the early-stage conversation.

  • Dry bulk and general cargo
  • Loading and discharge requirements
  • Laycan and freight enquiries
  • Commercial term clarification
SERVICE / 02

Bunker Broking & Marine Fuels

Assisting buyers with marine fuel enquiries by structuring the request and communicating with relevant supply contacts. Quotations, supply, specifications and payment conditions depend on the supplier, port and vessel.

  • VLSFO, HSFO and marine gasoil enquiries
  • Ports, ETA and delivery method
  • Quantity, grades and specifications
  • Quotation and terms coordination
SERVICE / 03

Vessel Availability & Research

Reviewing publicly available and market-shared vessel information to help identify potential candidates aligned with the stated cargo requirements. Vessel position and availability must be reconfirmed with the controlling party.

  • Handysize to larger bulk tonnage
  • General cargo vessel enquiries
  • Open position and timing review
  • Vessel particulars and suitability
SERVICE / 04

Broker & Market Communication

Keeping enquiry details consistent, communicating with interested market contacts, and following up on the key information required to advance a commercial discussion.

  • Relevant market introductions
  • Enquiry and offer follow-up
  • Document and detail requests
  • Clear intermediary communication
Important: Nano Shipbroking acts in an independent intermediary capacity. Availability, quotations, counterparties, sanctions compliance and any final agreement are subject to proper verification and direct confirmation. We do not claim vessel ownership or guaranteed fuel supply.
